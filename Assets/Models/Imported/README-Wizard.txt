"Low Poly stylized wizard comes with a textured cloak, classic hat, and
a trusty staff." -- Denys Almaral

Used here as the real visual for the Elder NPC (Assets/Editor/
SceneBootstrapper.cs BuildElderTent/CreateElderNpc), replacing the
earlier primitive-box figure. Ships no animation of its own, so it
plays the same idle clip baked into
Assets/Models/Imported/PersonAnimated.fbx via Humanoid avatar
retargeting -- same trick used for the selectable player avatars,
just with a small dedicated Idle-only Animator Controller since this
NPC never moves.
